About

I didn't start out loving this field — for a long time, I barely understood it, and I can't say I enjoyed it either. At some point I decided that didn't matter: I wanted to build something real for myself regardless of how I felt about it day to day, so I kept going. I learned languages I'd never touched, broke code and put it back together, sat through courses that didn't always make sense the first time. Somewhere in that process — without a clear turning point I can point back to — it changed. I started being curious rather than just dutiful, and comfortable being the person still figuring things out.

I'm now a third-year Computer Science & AI student at Beni Suef National University, working across full-stack .NET development, cybersecurity, and database management. There's still a lot I don't know, and for the first time, that feels like something to look forward to rather than something to get through.
